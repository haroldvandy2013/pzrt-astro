<div class="source-gap-36"></div><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-96720e">We are a uniquely diverse group of people</span></span></strong>
<strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-020e14"> interested in learning from each other about our differences while creating issue-focused dramas.</span></span></strong>
</p>
<div class="source-gap-36"></div><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-96720e">We are Black, White, Latino, Asian, European, Middle Eastern, straight, gay, young and older.</span></span></strong>
<strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-020e14"> Our religions are as diverse as our ethnic, economic, professional and social backgrounds. </span></span></strong>
</p>
<div class="source-gap-36"></div><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-96720e">Our performances are designed to not only enlighten our audiences about things that should concern us all but demonstrate the bonds we’ve created and our mutual understanding of our shared existence.</span></span></strong>
</p>
<div class="source-gap-36"></div><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-020e14">We started as Drexel University Readers’ Theater in 2014 with </span></span></strong>
<strong><em><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-96720e">“Better to Light a Single Candle Than to Curse the Darkness.”</span></span></em>
</strong>
<strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-020e14"> It was an in-person performance and follow-up discussion on racism, homophobia, and antisemitism. </span></span></strong>
</p>
