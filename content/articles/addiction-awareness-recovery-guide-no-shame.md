<h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><strong><span class="source-background-fbd95b">Addiction </span></strong>
<span class="source-background-fbd95b">is a complex health condition—not a choice or a failure. </span><span class="source-size-24">This guide is created with the </span><strong><span class="source-size-24">NO SHAME</span></strong>
<span class="source-size-24"> spirit: to help you understand addictive substances, recognize causes, explore treatment, and support yourself or loved ones on the path to recovery.</span></h2>
<div class="source-gap-39_203125"></div><h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-86e5d1">Is addiction a </span><strong><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-86e5d1">disease</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-86e5d1">?</span></h2>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">Yes, addiction is a disease. </span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">The American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM) defines addiction as a </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">chronic brain disorder</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">.</span></span></p>

<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">Substances </span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">can be </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">prescription medications</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff"> or </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">non-medical drugs</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff"> or </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">alcohol </span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">and </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">include</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff"> </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">many thi</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841 source-background-ffffff">ngs you are probably familiar with.</span></span></p>

</li>
<li class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><strong><span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">Tobacco/nicotine</span></span></strong>
</p>
</li>
<li class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">Caffeine</span></span></p>

<li class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><strong><span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">Cannabis (marijuana)</span></span></strong>

<li class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><strong><span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">Prescription and non-prescription opioids</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">, such as codeine, oxycodone and heroin</span></span></p>
</li>
<li class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-27"><strong><span class="source-size-18"><span class="source-color-000000">Prescription and non-prescription stimulants</span></span></strong>

<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">Use of these substances can lead to substance use disorders (SUDs</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">) — but NOT always. SUDs can be mild, moderate or severe. </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">Addiction</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841"> is the most </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">severe</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841"> form of a SUD.</span></span></p>

<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">Symptoms of addiction</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841"> vary from </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">person to person</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841"> </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">based on the substance</span></span></strong>
<span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841"> or the </span></span><strong><span class="source-size-20"><span class="source-color-0e2841">activity.</span></span></strong>
</p>
<img src="/media/wix-a76bb0_efcd0b82ff3842708b82a0b8a147cb42-mv2.jpeg" alt="" width="740" height="416"><div class="source-gap-36"></div><h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><span class="source-color-000000 source-background-86e5d1">Causes Of Addiction</span></h2>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Genetics:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Studies show that genetic factors are responsible for </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">40% to 60%</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> of the vulnerability to any SUD. If you have a first-degree relative (biological sibling or parent) with a substance abuse disorder, you’re more likely to develop one. Scientists are working to locate specific genes that may contribute to this vulnerability.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Mental health conditions:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> There’s a strong link between addiction and mental health conditions, such as </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">depression, PTSD and bipolar disorder</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">. About half of the people who experience a mental health condition will also experience a substance abuse disorder SUD and vice versa.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Environmental factors:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">Access to substances</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> is a particularly significant environmental risk factor. Factors include substance use by household or peers, misuse‑prone prescriptions, and </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">adverse childhood experiences (ACEs)</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">. ACEs are strongly related to addiction and lifelong health problems.</span></p>
<img src="/media/wix-a76bb0_6e3c48a72fb54abcbde91bfe1b127f0d-mv2.jpeg" alt="" width="740" height="416"><div class="source-gap-36"></div><h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><span class="source-color-000000 source-background-86e5d1">Treatment Options</span></h2>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Hospital management:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Certain substances can create </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">dangerous withdrawal</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">. Special hospital units provide monitoring and care during withdrawal.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Medications:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Certain medications can </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">reduce urges, cravings and withdrawal symptoms</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">. Mental health conditions (bipolar, depression) may also be treated with medication.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Rehabilitation (rehab):</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Also called “sober living” or residential treatment. Offers </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">structured counseling, education, support</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">. Inpatient (live‑in) or outpatient (visit sessions). Focuses on </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">long‑term recovery</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Therapy:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Includes </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">cognitive behavioral therapy, group therapy</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> to gain new perspectives and change behavior.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Support groups:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Such as </span><strong><span class="source-color-0e2841">Alcoholics Anonymous ( AA), Narcotics Anonymous (NA),</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">Al‑Anon</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> (for families). Share experiences and provide ongoing support.</span></p>

<div class="source-gap-36"></div><h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><span class="source-color-000000 source-background-86e5d1">Steps for Reducing the Risk of Developing Addiction</span></h2>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Avoid or limit addictive substances:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Follow prescription rules; contact a provider if dependent.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Know your family history:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Higher vulnerability with family SUD; discuss risk reduction with a doctor.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Manage stress healthily:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Use </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">exercise, meditation, hobbies</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000">; seek mental health support if needed.</span></p>
<div class="source-gap-36"></div><h2 class="source-size-28 source-face-barlow-medium source-weight-400 source-line-56"><span class="source-color-000000 source-background-86e5d1">Tips to Help Yourself / Loved One</span></h2>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Speak up sooner rather than later:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Earlier treatment = better outcomes. Help find help without judgment.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Practice empathy:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Listen thoughtfully; build trust.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Be patient:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Addiction is a </span><strong><span class="source-color-000000">chronic condition</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> with no quick fix.</span></p>
<p class="source-size-18 source-face-barlow-extralight source-weight-400 source-line-36"><strong><span class="source-color-000000">• Take care of yourself:</span></strong>
<span class="source-color-000000"> Families often feel stress/grief; prioritize your mental health.</span></p>
